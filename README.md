## What Aivion is

Aivion is a workplace for Windows for people who build with AI agents. Claude Code and Codex are built in, each in a tab of its own, in your project. You read along and steer where you want; with both, the primary agent hands work to the other, in view.

- **Your agents in one place.** Claude Code, Codex, or both. Choose the model and how deeply it thinks, next to its name.
- **Plan as you think.** Notes, a numbered to-do list and a drawing board, which the agents read. Say "do #4": the agent tells its plan first, and stops along the way so that you can test.
- **See it live.** Your site or game next to the conversation, as a phone, a tablet or full screen.
- **Your projects in order.** In groups, with GitHub at hand, and an eye on passwords and keys that would go along with your code.
- **Know what it costs.** How much of your subscriptions the agents used, the pace, and how long they worked per project.
- **Set up in one go.** What building with agents takes and is not there yet, Git, Node.js, Laravel Herd and the agents you choose, is installed from their makers, step by step.

Your work stays on your computer. Aivion reads no conversation of your agents, and your notes and to-do list stay out of git.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11.
- One agent is enough:
  - **Claude Code**, with a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) or an Anthropic API key;
  - **Codex**, with a ChatGPT plan such as Plus, or an OpenAI API key;
  - or both, which then work together.

## Getting Aivion

Aivion is bought at **[aivion.app](https://aivion.app/en/pricing/)**: €24,99 per year, VAT included. It is not renewed automatically, and all updates are included while your licence runs.

After paying you download Aivion at once, from the thank-you page, the mail, or your account at [mijn.aivion.app](https://mijn.aivion.app). New versions come by themselves: Aivion tells what is new, and asks whether to update now, later, or not this one.

## This repository

This repository holds the installers of Aivion, one release per version, which the app uses for its updates. It holds no source code. To install Aivion, use the download in your account: an installer from here asks for a licence too.

Questions or problems? Mail [support@aivion.app](mailto:support@aivion.app).
